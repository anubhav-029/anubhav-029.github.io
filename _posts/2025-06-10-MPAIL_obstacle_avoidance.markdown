We constrain reward function candidacy during Inverse Reinforcement Learning (IRL) to the space of Control Barrier Functions (CBFs), enabling data-driven recovery of barrier functions directly from unlabeled expert observations for safe online control in simulation and real-world obstacle avoidance.
